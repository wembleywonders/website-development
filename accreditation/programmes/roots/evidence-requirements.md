# Roots — Evidence Requirements

## What Counts as Evidence

Evidence must be authentic (created by the learner), sufficient (covers all
criteria), current (produced during or after programme enrolment), and valid
(directly demonstrates the criterion).

A distinctive point for this programme: **a nil result is evidence.** The
research log records every source consulted, including the ones that returned
nothing, because the pattern of absence is itself a finding (ROOTS-1 1.3,
ROOTS-2 2.5, the Module 6 skill).

## Storage and Submission

All evidence stored in the member's ILP. Evidence portfolio submitted via
platform assessment request. Assessor reviews within 10 working days. For
ROOTS-2P, the Scrap Cat co-assessor reviews the craft criteria in the same
window and both sign-offs are recorded against the unit.

## Third-Party Consent and Screening (ROOTS-2 / ROOTS-3)

This programme's evidence routinely records or names people other than the
learner. Before any interview evidence (2.2) or deposit (3.1) is submitted:

- the person interviewed must have given informed consent, as a process, to
  being recorded and to each intended use (recording, transcription, deposit,
  WW commercial use) — a signature alone is not sufficient evidence of consent;
- where the interview content or a written-up account **names a further living
  person who did not themselves consent** (a relative described in the
  account), the **protected-tier screening** in the Knowledge Commons consent
  model applies before any deposit. A Knowledge Commons deposit is not a
  condition of passing 2.2, but the screening **is** a condition of depositing;
- material that is **documented and true but harmful to disclose** (an
  undisclosed parentage, a criminal record, an institutionalisation, an
  immigration irregularity) is treated as the subject's disclosure to make, not
  the researcher's — held or redacted per the screening note (2.4 / 7.1).

## Authenticity Declarations

Member signs an authenticity declaration for each unit submission confirming:

- The research and writing are their own;
- Any collaboration is clearly identified — including the person recorded in an
  interview, who is **credited, not co-assessed**, and for ROOTS-2P the Scrap
  Cat co-assessor's contribution to the craft sign-off;
- AI-assisted work is declared and the analytical and interpretive decisions
  were theirs;
- The research log is complete — sources that returned nothing are recorded, not
  omitted;
- For the capstone: the standing of the research is stated honestly, and a wall
  is presented as a wall.
