### Unit Content Mapping (narrative modules → RQF units)

**TNB-1 — Music Theory, Composition & Sound Production (RQF L2, 4 credits)**
Draws on: Module 1 (Music Theory & Composition Fundamentals), Module 2
(Beat-Making & Production Technique — BeatMakerROV), Module 3 (Sound
Engineering & Audio Quality — AudioBay quality-check gate). Curator grounding:
W.C. Handy (transcription/codification), Son House (direct technique
transmission), King Tubby (remix/deconstruction — this unit's clearest
real-tool fit, since AudioBay's quality-check gate is literally a
mixing/engineering assessment).

**TNB-2 — Collaborative Practice, Artist Development & Live Performance (RQF
L2, 4 credits)** Draws on: Module 4 (Genre Exploration & Cultural Roots),
Module 5 (Live Performance & Set Practice — Carnival sound-clash line),
Module 6 (Collaboration & Ensemble Practice — Streetbeats band model), Module
9 (Artist Development & A&R Practice — NEW, added 20 Aug 2026). Curator
grounding: Art Blakey (bandleading-as-apprenticeship), Lord Kitchener
(tent-based sustained mentorship), Coxsone Dodd (artist development/A&R — the
gap this module exists to close), Cissy Houston (vocal coaching), Sister
Rosetta Tharpe (concrete platform-giving to a named newcomer), Maurice White
(holistic one-on-one mentorship). Module 9 content: running a structured
audition/discovery process; coaching a raw performer toward a finished,
presentable act; matching a developing artist to the right collaborators
within the platform. Builder+ evidence: documented development of another
member's raw work into something presentable — not a solo output, an act of
facilitation, verified via the platform's existing peer-witness requirement
(the developed member's own confirmation is the evidence). This also
satisfies the Crew Log's "peer-facing showcase" touchpoint from the
cross-skill rotation gate (see ww-skunkworks-impact-labs.md) — Trubble n Bass
previously had no module producing that touchpoint explicitly.

**TNB-3 — Distribution, Airplay & Original Production Capstone (RQF L2, 3
credits)** Draws on: Module 7 (Distribution & Airplay — Afua/Lady Cynthia's
gatekeeper role, direct Rayd-yo pairing), Module 8 (Original Track/Set
Production — capstone, portfolio via Joystick). Curator grounding: Bernard
Edwards (groove/bassline architecture as a foundational-element case study),
Jam Master Jay (reciprocal skill exchange + structured teaching institution),
Terror Danjah (broadcast/media platforming).
