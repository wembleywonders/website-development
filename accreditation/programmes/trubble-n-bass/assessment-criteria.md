# Trubble n Bass — Assessment Criteria

## Programme Overview
Trubble n Bass develops music composition, production, artist development, and distribution skills across 19 genre traditions (jazz, blues, dub/reggae, classical, gospel, soul, calypso, soca, funk, disco, rock/rock and roll, R&B, hip-hop, lovers rock, jungle/drum & bass, UK garage, highlife, afrobeat, afrobeats), each with a named real historical curator. Assessment is competence-based — creators demonstrate skills through real outputs, not examinations.

## Assessment Areas

### Unit TNB-1: Music Theory, Composition & Sound Production (Level 2, 4 credits)

**Learning Outcomes:**
1. Understand music theory, composition, and notation fundamentals
2. Produce a beat or track using BeatMakerROV
3. Apply sound engineering and remix/deconstruction technique
4. Pass AudioBay's quality-check gate

**Assessment Criteria:**

| Criterion | Evidence Required | Pass Standard |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1.1 Notate or compose an original piece | Notated transcription or original composition | Correctly notated; if transcribed from an oral/folk source, source is credited (W.C. Handy grounding) |
| 1.2 Produce a beat or track | BeatMakerROV output file | Complete arrangement, tempo/key stated |
| 1.3 Apply remix/deconstruction technique | Remix of an existing track (own work or cleared for use) | Demonstrates stripping to rhythm section and rebuilding (King Tubby grounding) |
| 1.4 Pass AudioBay quality-check | AudioBay submission with reviewer sign-off | All technical checklist items (levels/format/clearance/mix) checked, none flagged, human-approved advance |

### Unit TNB-2: Collaborative Practice, Artist Development & Live Performance (Level 2, 4 credits)

**Learning Outcomes:**
1. Research and present a genre's cultural roots
2. Perform live in a defined ensemble role
3. Sustain collaborative ensemble practice
4. Develop another member's raw talent into a presentable act (Artist Development & A&R)

**Assessment Criteria:**

| Criterion | Evidence Required | Pass Standard |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| 2.1 Research and present a genre's cultural roots | Written or recorded presentation | Accurate, sourced, credits the tradition of origin |
| 2.2 Perform live in a defined ensemble role | Recorded or witnessed live performance | Role executed to a presentable standard, peer-witnessed |
| 2.3 Sustain collaborative ensemble practice | Documented rehearsal/collaboration log (Streetbeats band model) | Minimum 3 sessions, sustained not one-off |
| 2.4 Develop another member's raw talent (Artist Development & A&R) | Documented development process, plus the developed member's own confirmation | Raw work demonstrably improved into a presentable act, confirmed by the developed member via peer witness (Coxsone Dodd grounding — the gap this criterion exists to close) |

### Unit TNB-3: Distribution, Airplay & Original Production Capstone (Level 2, 3 credits)

**Learning Outcomes:**
1. Prepare a track or set to distribution specification
2. Secure airplay or platform placement
3. Produce and publish an original capstone piece

**Assessment Criteria:**

| Criterion | Evidence Required | Pass Standard |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| 3.1 Prepare track/set to distribution spec | Final mix or master file | Meets Rayd-yo distribution spec sheet |
| 3.2 Submit for airplay/platform placement | Submission to Afua/Rayd-yo gatekeeper | Passes gatekeeper review, or documented referral feedback |
| 3.3 Produce an original capstone track/set | Complete original piece | Demonstrates skills from TNB-1 and TNB-2 combined |
| 3.4 Publish to Joystick portfolio | Live Joystick portfolio entry | Complete metadata, credits curators/techniques drawn on |

## Grading

All units assessed on Pass/Refer basis. No grades — competence is the standard.

**Referral:** Assessor provides written feedback. Creator has 20 working days to resubmit.

**Appeal:** Follow standard appeals procedure (see quality-assurance/appeals-procedure.md).

## Assessment Methods

- **Portfolio of evidence** — primary method. Creator compiles evidence in their ILP.
- **Observation** — assessor witnesses live performance or ensemble session.
- **Professional discussion** — assessor questions creator on process, decisions, and (for 2.4) the development relationship itself.

Successful completion of all three units = 11 credits at Level 2. Maps to:
- Ofqual Level 2 Certificate equivalent (pending formal recognition)
- Wembley Wonders Trubble n Bass Builder badge
