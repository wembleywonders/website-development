# Auntie Anansi's Kitchen — Evidence Requirements

## What Counts as Evidence

Evidence must be authentic (created by the learner), sufficient (covers all
criteria), current (produced during or after programme enrolment), and valid
(directly demonstrates the criterion).

### Acceptable Evidence Types

| Type | Description | Units Covered |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Food Safety certificate | Current Level 2 Award in Food Safety in Catering (RQF) | 1.1 |
| Food-safety knowledge check | Completed knowledge check where no certificate is held | 1.1 |
| Witnessed prep/cook session | Assessor observation record of a live cook | 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 |
| Finished-dish evidence | Photo or video of the finished dish plus written method notes | 1.2, 1.3, 1.4 |
| Jerk marinade + ingredient list | The marinade, its ingredient list, and the marinated result | 1.4 |
| Cultural-history account | Short written or recorded account of a technique's origin | 1.5, 2.1, 2.2 |
| Regional comparison | Written or recorded comparison of three or more island traditions | 2.1 |
| Calendar-food account | Written or recorded account of three or more calendar/ceremony foods | 2.2 |
| Recipe documentation package | Cook-along video + written-up recipe with story + source's sign-off | 2.3 |
| KC deposit consent record | Completed deposit licence + commercial-use opt-in + attribution + screening note | 2.4 |
| Dish costing sheet | Per-portion costing with hidden costs, waste factor, food-cost % and price | 3.1 |
| Trading plan | Market choice, legal checklist, equipment list, menu + allergen matrix | 3.2 |
| Trading-event evidence | Photos, sales record and temperature logs from a real stall / supper club / pop-up | 3.3 |
| Post-event review | Written review of sales, margin achieved and changes for next time | 3.4 |

## Storage and Submission

All evidence stored in the member's ILP. Evidence portfolio submitted via
platform assessment request. Assessor reviews within 10 working days.

## Third-Party Consent (AAK-2 documentation evidence)

A recipe documentation package (2.3) records another person. Before it is
submitted:
- the person cooked with and recorded must have given informed consent to
  being documented and to the intended use of the recording;
- if the written-up recipe or its story names a further living person who
  did not themselves consent (a relative referenced in the account), the
  protected-tier screening in the Knowledge Commons consent model applies
  before any deposit — a Knowledge Commons deposit is not a condition of
  passing 2.3, but the screening is a condition of depositing.

## Authenticity Declarations

Member signs an authenticity declaration for each unit submission confirming:
- Work is their own
- Any collaboration is clearly identified (including the person recorded in a
  documentation package, who is credited, not co-assessed)
- AI-assisted content is declared and the creative and craft decisions were theirs
- For a documented recipe: the source has seen and approved the written-up version
