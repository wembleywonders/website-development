# Internal Verification Procedures

## Overview

Internal verification ensures that assessment decisions are consistent, fair, and meet OCN standards. This document outlines IV procedures for Wembley Wonders CIC.

---

## Internal Quality Assurer (IQA) Role

### Responsibilities

- Sample assessment decisions
- Standardise assessment practice
- Support assessor development
- Maintain quality records
- Prepare for external verification

### Qualifications Required

- **V1/IQA qualification** or equivalent
- Subject knowledge across programmes
- Experience as an assessor
- DBS Enhanced certificate

---

## Sampling Strategy

### Sample Size

- **Minimum 25%** of all assessments
- **100%** of first-time assessors' decisions
- **100%** of any assessment queried

### Sampling Criteria

| Priority | Description |
| ---------- | ------------- |
| High | New assessors, new units, appeals |
| Medium | Complex evidence, borderline decisions |
| Low | Experienced assessors, straightforward evidence |

---

## IV Process

### 1. Planning

- Create IV sampling plan (start of each cohort)
- Identify assessments to be sampled
- Schedule IV activities

### 2. Sampling

- Review assessment records
- Examine learner evidence
- Check assessment decision against criteria
- Verify feedback quality

### 3. Recording

- Complete IV record form
- Document findings and actions
- Provide feedback to assessor

### 4. Standardisation

- Hold quarterly standardisation meetings
- Share good practice
- Address inconsistencies
- Update guidance as needed

---

## IV Decision Outcomes

| Outcome | Action |
| --------- | -------- |
| **Agree** | Assessment decision confirmed |
| **Agree with development points** | Minor improvements suggested |
| **Disagree** | Reassessment required |

## Standardisation Meetings

### Frequency

- Quarterly (minimum)
- Additional meetings as needed

### Agenda

1. Review assessment samples
2. Discuss borderline cases
3. Share good practice
4. Address consistency issues
5. Update documentation
6. Plan for next period

### Attendance

- All active assessors (mandatory)
- IQA (chair)
- Centre lead (as needed)

---

| Record Type | Retention Period |
| ------------- | ----------------- |
| IV records | 3 years post-completion |
| Standardisation minutes | 3 years |
| Assessment records | 3 years post-completion |
| Learner evidence | 3 years post-completion |
