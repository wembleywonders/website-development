# External Verification Procedures

## Overview

External verification (EV) is conducted by OCN London to ensure Wembley Wonders CIC maintains quality standards. This document outlines EV preparation and processes.

---

## External Verifier Role

OCN London appoints External Verifiers to:

- Verify centre assessment practices
- Sample assessed learner work
- Confirm qualification claims
- Provide development feedback
- Report on centre performance

---

## EV Visit Types

| Visit Type | Frequency | Purpose |
| ------------ | ----------- | --------- |
| **Centre Approval** | One-time | Initial centre approval |
| **Annual Monitoring** | Yearly | Ongoing quality assurance |
| **Sampling** | As scheduled | Remote evidence sampling |
| **Ad-hoc** | As needed | Issue investigation |

---

## Preparation Checklist

### Before EV Visit

#### Documentation Ready

- ☐ Centre policies and procedures
- ☐ Assessor CVs and qualifications
- ☐ IQA qualifications
- ☐ IV sampling records
- ☐ Standardisation meeting minutes
- ☐ Assessment records
- ☐ Learner evidence portfolios

### Logistics

- ☐ Quiet meeting space arranged
- ☐ IT access for online evidence
- ☐ Staff availability confirmed
- ☐ Learner availability (if interviews)
- ☐ Refreshments available

#### Evidence Organisation

- ☐ Sample learners identified
- ☐ Evidence accessible
- ☐ IV records attached
- ☐ Tracking documents ready

## Evidence Sampling

### What EV Will Review

- Assessed learner portfolios
- Assessment records and decisions
- IV sampling records
- Feedback given to learners
- Appeals records (if any)

### Sample Selection

EV will typically sample:

- Range of assessors
- Range of programmes/units
- Pass and refer decisions
- First-time learners
- Any flagged concerns

---

## EV Report Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --------- | --------- |
| **High Confidence** | Excellent quality, minimal actions |
| **Reasonable Confidence** | Good quality, some actions |
| **Limited Confidence** | Issues found, actions required |
| **No Confidence** | Serious issues, sanctions possible |

---

## Action Plan Response

If actions identified:

1. **Receive report** (within 10 working days)
2. **Review actions** with team
3. **Create action plan** with deadlines
4. **Submit response** to OCN London
5. **Implement actions**
6. **Provide evidence** of completion
7. **Follow-up** at next EV

---

## Sanctions

Possible sanctions for non-compliance:

- Additional EV visits
- Increased sampling
- Suspension of registration
- Withdrawal of centre approval
