# Digital Support Technician — Level 3 Apprenticeship Pathway

## Standard Overview

**Standard**: Digital Support Technician  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 18 months  
**EPA**: End Point Assessment  

## Wembley Wonders Alignment

Learners completing **STEMgineers** OCN Certificate are prepared for this apprenticeship.

---

## Progression Route

```
STEMgineers Certificate (Level 2)
        ↓
Gap-filling modules (2-3 weeks)
        ↓
Apprenticeship application
        ↓
Digital Support Technician (Level 3, 18 months)
        ↓
Career: Digital Support, IT Analyst, Technical Support
```

---

## Alternative Pathways

STEMgineers also prepares learners for:
- **Software Developer** (Level 4) - with additional coding
- **Data Analyst** (Level 4) - with additional data skills
- **IT Support Technician** (Level 3) - via Scrap Cat crossover

EPA components:
1. **Work-based Project**: Technical project in workplace
2. **Portfolio**: Evidence of competence
3. **Professional Discussion**: Interview with assessor
