# Business Administrator — Level 3 Apprenticeship Pathway

## Standard Overview

**Standard**: Business Administrator  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 18 months  
**EPA**: End Point Assessment  

## Wembley Wonders Alignment

Learners completing **TECHreneurs** OCN Certificate are prepared for this apprenticeship.

## Gap Analysis

| Gap | How to Address |
|-----|----------------|
| Policies/regulations | Additional module |
| Formal meeting support | Work placement |
| Administrative projects | Structured project |

---

## Progression Route

```
TECHreneurs Certificate (Level 2)
        ↓
Gap-filling modules (2-3 weeks)
        ↓
Apprenticeship application
        ↓
Business Administrator (Level 3, 18 months)
        ↓
Career: Business Admin, Office Manager, Project Coordinator
```

## End Point Assessment

EPA components:
1. **Knowledge Test**: Multiple choice and short answer
2. **Portfolio-based Interview**: Discussion of evidence
3. **Project Presentation**: Work-based project
