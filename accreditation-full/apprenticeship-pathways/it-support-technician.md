# IT Support Technician — Level 3 Apprenticeship Pathway

## Standard Overview

**Standard**: IT Support Technician  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 18 months (typical)  
**EPA**: End Point Assessment  

## Wembley Wonders Alignment

Learners completing **Scrap Cat** OCN Certificate are prepared for this apprenticeship.

---

## Gap Analysis

| Gap | How to Address |
|-----|----------------|
| IT Security | Additional module/workshop |
| Networking | Additional module/workshop |
| Remote Support | Work placement experience |
| Customer Service | Extended Repair Café experience |

---

## Progression Route

```
Scrap Cat Certificate (Level 2)
        ↓
Gap-filling modules (2-4 weeks)
        ↓
Apprenticeship application
        ↓
IT Support Technician (Level 3, 18 months)
        ↓
Career: IT Support, Helpdesk, Technical Support
```

---

## Employer Partners

Potential employer partners for apprenticeship:
- Currys (Brent Cross)
- Local IT repair shops
- Council IT departments
- NHS Digital
- School IT teams
- SME IT support providers

---

## End Point Assessment

EPA components:
1. **Project**: Workplace-based technical project
2. **Portfolio**: Evidence of competence
3. **Professional Discussion**: Interview with assessor
