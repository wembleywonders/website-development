# Content Creator — Level 3 Apprenticeship Pathway

## Standard Overview

**Standard**: Content Creator  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 15-18 months  
**EPA**: End Point Assessment  

## Wembley Wonders Alignment

Learners completing **G-Tech Casters** OCN Certificate are prepared for this apprenticeship.

## Gap Analysis

| Gap | How to Address |
|-----|----------------|
| Copyright law | Additional workshop |

---

## Progression Route

```
G-Tech Casters Certificate (Level 2)
        ↓
Gap-filling modules (1-2 weeks)
        ↓
Apprenticeship application
        ↓
Content Creator (Level 3, 15-18 months)
        ↓
Career: Content Creator, Social Media Manager, Podcast Producer
```

## End Point Assessment

EPA components:
1. **Portfolio**: Collection of work produced
2. **Content Production Project**: Brief-based creation
3. **Professional Discussion**: Interview with assessor
