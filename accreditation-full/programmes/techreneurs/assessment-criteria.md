# 💰 TECHreneurs — Assessment Criteria

## Overview

This document defines the assessment criteria for each badge level in the TECHreneurs programme. Assessors use these criteria to evaluate learner evidence and determine badge readiness.

---

## Badge 1: Business Canvas (Explorer)

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this badge, learners will be able to:
1. Generate and evaluate business ideas
2. Complete a Business Model Canvas
3. Define target customers and value propositions
4. Present a business idea confidently

### Assessment Criteria

| Ref | Criterion | Evidence Required | Assessor Checklist |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| BC1.1 | Generate 3+ business ideas | Ideation worksheet | ☐ Ideas documented |
| BC1.2 | Evaluate ideas against criteria | Evaluation matrix | ☐ Criteria applied |
| BC1.3 | Select most viable idea | Justification statement | ☐ Reasoning clear |
| BC2.1 | Complete all 9 Canvas sections | Business Model Canvas | ☐ All sections filled |
| BC2.2 | Customer Segments defined | Canvas section | ☐ Clear definition |
| BC2.3 | Value Proposition articulated | Canvas section | ☐ Compelling proposition |
| BC2.4 | Revenue Streams identified | Canvas section | ☐ Realistic streams |
| BC2.5 | Key Resources listed | Canvas section | ☐ Resources identified |
| BC3.1 | Create customer persona | Persona document | ☐ Detailed persona |
| BC3.2 | Identify customer pain points | Persona document | ☐ Pain points listed |
| BC3.3 | Match solution to pain points | Value prop statement | ☐ Clear alignment |
| BC4.1 | Prepare 60-second pitch | Script/notes | ☐ Prepared |
| BC4.2 | Deliver pitch confidently | Recording/observation | ☐ Clear delivery |
| BC4.3 | Answer follow-up questions | Q&A session | ☐ Handled questions |
| BC4.4 | Incorporate feedback | Revised pitch | ☐ Improvements made |

### Business Model Canvas Standards

Canvas must demonstrate:
- Logical consistency between sections
- Realistic assumptions
- Evidence of customer focus
- Clear value proposition
- Viable revenue model

## Badge 2: Market Researcher (Builder)

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this badge, learners will be able to:
1. Design and conduct primary research
2. Analyse competitive landscape
3. Develop pricing strategies
4. Create basic financial projections

### Assessment Criteria

| Ref | Criterion | Evidence Required | Assessor Checklist |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| MR1.1 | Design research methodology | Research plan | ☐ Clear methodology |
| MR1.2 | Create survey/interview questions | Question set | ☐ Unbiased questions |
| MR1.3 | Collect 10+ survey responses OR 3+ interviews | Raw data | ☐ Quantity met |
| MR1.4 | Analyse research findings | Analysis report | ☐ Insights extracted |
| MR1.5 | Apply findings to business model | Updated Canvas | ☐ Changes made |
| MR2.1 | Identify 3+ direct competitors | Competitor list | ☐ Competitors found |
| MR2.2 | Analyse competitor strengths | Matrix/analysis | ☐ Strengths identified |
| MR2.3 | Analyse competitor weaknesses | Matrix/analysis | ☐ Weaknesses found |
| MR2.4 | Identify competitive advantage | Statement | ☐ Differentiation clear |
| MR3.1 | Calculate all costs | Cost breakdown | ☐ Costs identified |
| MR3.2 | Research market pricing | Pricing research | ☐ Market data gathered |
| MR3.3 | Develop pricing strategy | Pricing document | ☐ Strategy justified |
| MR3.4 | Calculate profit margins | Calculations | ☐ Margins realistic |
| MR4.1 | Project monthly revenue | Spreadsheet | ☐ 6-month projection |
| MR4.2 | Project monthly costs | Spreadsheet | ☐ Costs projected |
| MR4.3 | Calculate break-even point | Calculation | ☐ Break-even shown |

### Research Quality Standards

Primary research must demonstrate:
- Ethical data collection (informed consent)
- Unbiased question design
- Representative sample (for target market)
- Systematic analysis
- Actionable insights

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this badge, learners will be able to:
1. Launch products/services on e-commerce platforms
2. Generate actual revenue
3. Execute marketing campaigns
4. Manage customer relationships

### Assessment Criteria

| Ref | Criterion | Evidence Required | Assessor Checklist |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| EN1.1 | Create Cyberstore listing | Live listing | ☐ Listing active |
| EN1.2 | Write compelling product description | Listing copy | ☐ Engaging copy |
| EN1.3 | Set appropriate pricing | Listing | ☐ Price justified |
| EN1.4 | Include quality images | Listing images | ☐ Professional images |
| EN1.5 | Configure payment/delivery | Listing settings | ☐ Settings correct |
| EN2.1 | Make first sale | Transaction evidence | ☐ Sale completed |
| EN2.2 | Process order correctly | Order record | ☐ Order fulfilled |
| EN2.3 | Handle customer enquiry | Communication log | ☐ Professional response |
| EN2.4 | Collect customer feedback | Feedback/review | ☐ Feedback received |
| EN3.1 | Create marketing plan | Plan document | ☐ Plan comprehensive |
| EN3.2 | Design marketing content | Content files | ☐ Quality content |
| EN3.3 | Execute social media campaign | Social posts | ☐ Campaign live |
| EN3.4 | Track marketing performance | Analytics | ☐ Data collected |
| EN3.5 | Adjust based on results | Updated plan | ☐ Iteration shown |
| EN4.1 | Maintain customer records | CRM/spreadsheet | ☐ Records maintained |
| EN4.2 | Follow up with customers | Communication evidence | ☐ Follow-up done |
| EN4.3 | Handle complaint professionally | Case record | ☐ Issue resolved |

### Launch Quality Standards

Cyberstore listing must include:
- Clear, accurate product/service title
- Detailed description (benefits, features, terms)
- High-quality images (minimum 3)
- Accurate pricing and delivery information
- Contact/communication method
- Professional presentation

## Badge 4: Business Leader (Leader)

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this badge, learners will be able to:
1. Present business to external audiences
2. Mentor new entrepreneurs
3. Scale business operations
4. Demonstrate financial management

### Assessment Criteria

| Ref | Criterion | Evidence Required | Assessor Checklist |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| BL1.1 | Create full business plan | Business plan | ☐ Comprehensive plan |
| BL1.2 | Prepare PitchFest presentation | Slide deck | ☐ Professional deck |
| BL1.3 | Present at PitchFest | Recording/observation | ☐ Confident delivery |
| BL1.4 | Handle investor-style questions | Q&A evidence | ☐ Questions handled |
| BL1.5 | Incorporate feedback | Post-pitch reflection | ☐ Learning shown |
| BL2.1 | Mentor 3 new TECHreneurs | Mentoring logs | ☐ 3 mentees |
| BL2.2 | Guide mentees through Canvas | Mentee Canvases | ☐ Canvases completed |
| BL2.3 | Provide constructive feedback | Feedback examples | ☐ Quality feedback |
| BL2.4 | Adapt to mentee needs | Reflection | ☐ Flexibility shown |
| BL3.1 | Achieve £500+ revenue OR 50+ customers | Evidence | ☐ Target met |
| BL3.2 | Demonstrate consistent sales | Sales record | ☐ Multiple sales |
| BL3.3 | Identify growth opportunities | Growth plan | ☐ Opportunities found |
| BL3.4 | Plan for scaling | Scale strategy | ☐ Strategy viable |
| BL4.1 | Maintain accurate financial records | Records | ☐ Records complete |
| BL4.2 | Calculate profit/loss | P&L statement | ☐ Accurate calculation |
| BL4.3 | Make data-driven decisions | Decision log | ☐ Evidence-based |

### Business Plan Standards

Full business plan must include:
1. Executive Summary
2. Business Description
3. Market Analysis
4. Competitive Analysis
5. Marketing Strategy
6. Operations Plan
7. Financial Projections (12 months)
8. Growth Strategy
9. Risk Assessment
10. Team/Resources

## PitchFest Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Developing (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|-----------|---------------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Problem Definition** | Crystal clear, compelling | Clear and relevant | Somewhat clear | Unclear/vague |
| **Solution** | Innovative, well-defined | Clear, logical | Basic understanding | Poorly defined |
| **Market Opportunity** | Data-backed, large opportunity | Good research | Limited research | No evidence |
| **Business Model** | Viable, scalable | Realistic | Needs refinement | Not viable |
| **Traction** | Strong evidence | Some evidence | Limited evidence | No evidence |
| **Delivery** | Confident, engaging | Clear, professional | Somewhat confident | Needs practice |
| **Q&A Handling** | Expert responses | Good answers | Basic responses | Struggled |

**Pass threshold**: Average score of 2.5+ across all criteria

---

## Reasonable Adjustments

Assessors may apply reasonable adjustments including:
- Written alternatives to verbal presentations
- Extended time for research activities
- Alternative evidence formats
- Assistive technology support
- Flexible assessment scheduling
- Supported pitch delivery

All adjustments must be documented and approved by the IQA.

---

## Revenue Verification

For Entrepreneur and Business Leader badges:
- Sales must be genuine (not friends/family unless documented)
- Revenue evidence must include transaction records
- Assessor may verify through Cyberstore analytics
- Cash sales require receipt evidence
