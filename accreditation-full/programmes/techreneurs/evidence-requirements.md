# 💰 TECHreneurs — Evidence Requirements

## Overview

This guide explains what evidence learners need to collect for each badge and how to submit it for assessment.

---

## Evidence Collection Principles

1. **Authenticity**: Business must be your own venture
2. **Verifiability**: Revenue and customers must be verifiable
3. **Currency**: Evidence should reflect current business state
4. **Ethics**: All business practices must be legal and ethical

---

### Pitch Guidelines

60-second pitch structure:
- **Hook** (5 seconds): Grab attention
- **Problem** (10 seconds): What problem exists?
- **Solution** (15 seconds): What's your solution?
- **Why You** (10 seconds): Why are you the right person?
- **Traction** (10 seconds): What have you achieved?
- **Ask** (10 seconds): What do you need/want?

| Analytics Data | Performance metrics | Screenshots | Multiple |
| Customer Feedback | Reviews/testimonials | Screenshots/forms | 1+ |
| Customer Records | CRM or database | Spreadsheet | 1 |

### Cyberstore Listing Checklist

Your listing must include:
- ☐ Clear product/service title
- ☐ Detailed description (what, how, terms)
- ☐ High-quality images (minimum 3)
- ☐ Accurate pricing
- ☐ Delivery/fulfilment information
- ☐ Contact method
- ☐ Category correctly selected
- ☐ Tags/keywords added

## Badge 4: Business Leader (Leader)

### Required Evidence

| Evidence Type | Description | Format | Quantity |
|---------------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Full Business Plan | Comprehensive document | PDF | 1 |
| PitchFest Presentation | Slide deck | PDF/PPTX | 1 |
| Pitch Recording | PitchFest performance | Video | 1 |
| PitchFest Feedback | Judges' comments | Form | 1 |
| Mentoring Logs | Session records | PDF | 3 logs |
| Mentee Canvases | Canvases you helped create | PDF | 3 |
| Mentee Feedback | Feedback from mentees | Forms | 3 |
| Revenue Evidence | £500+ or 50+ customers | Multiple | As needed |
| Financial Records | P&L, transactions | Spreadsheet | 1 |
| Growth Plan | Future strategy | PDF | 1 |
| Decision Log | Data-driven decisions | PDF | 1 |

### Business Plan Template

```
BUSINESS PLAN

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 page)
- Business name and concept
- Mission statement
- Key objectives
- Financial summary

2. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION (1-2 pages)
- What is the business?
- Products/services offered
- Business model
- Legal structure

3. MARKET ANALYSIS (2-3 pages)
- Industry overview
- Target market
- Market size and trends
- Customer analysis

4. COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS (1-2 pages)
- Main competitors
- Competitive advantages
- Market positioning

5. MARKETING STRATEGY (2-3 pages)
- Marketing objectives
- Target audience
- Marketing channels
- Pricing strategy
- Promotion plan

6. OPERATIONS PLAN (1-2 pages)
- How the business operates
- Key processes
- Suppliers/partners
- Technology/tools

7. FINANCIAL PROJECTIONS (2-3 pages)
- Revenue forecast (12 months)
- Cost projections
- Profit & Loss projection
- Cash flow forecast
- Break-even analysis

8. GROWTH STRATEGY (1 page)
- Short-term goals (6 months)
- Medium-term goals (1 year)
- Long-term vision (3 years)
- Scaling plans

9. RISK ASSESSMENT (1 page)
- Key risks identified
- Mitigation strategies
- Contingency plans

10. TEAM/RESOURCES (1 page)
- Key people involved
- Skills and roles
- Resource requirements
- Support network
```

### Revenue Verification Requirements

To prove £500+ revenue OR 50+ customers:

**For revenue:**
- Transaction screenshots from Cyberstore
- Bank statement excerpts (personal info redacted)
- Sales spreadsheet with dates

**For customers:**
- Customer list (names can be initials)
- Order records
- Communication evidence

Note: Friends/family purchases should be clearly marked and should not constitute majority of sales.
