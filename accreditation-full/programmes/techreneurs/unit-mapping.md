# 💰 TECHreneurs — OCN Unit Mapping Document

## Programme Overview

**Programme Name**: TECHreneurs  
**Tagline**: "Ideas. Income. Impact."  
**Target Age**: 14-24  
**Delivery Mode**: Blended (workshops + online hub + marketplace)  
**Platform Integration**: Cyberstore (55% revenue share)  

## Qualification Pathway

```
Business Canvas Badge (Explorer)
        ↓
Market Researcher Badge (Builder)
        ↓
Entrepreneur Badge (Innovator)
        ↓
Business Leader Badge (Leader)
        ↓
OCN Award in Business Enterprise (Level 1) — 6 credits
        ↓
OCN Certificate in Business Administration (Level 2) — 18 credits
        ↓
Business Administrator Apprenticeship (Level 3)
```
