## Performance Standards

All performances assessed on:
- **Presence**: Stage confidence, physical commitment
- **Voice**: Projection, clarity, emotional range
- **Character**: Consistency, believability, choices
- **Collaboration**: Ensemble awareness, generosity
- **Growth**: Development from rehearsal to performance
