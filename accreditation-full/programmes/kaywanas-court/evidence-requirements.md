## Oral History Recording Guidelines

When documenting elder stories:
- Obtain informed consent
- Record in comfortable environment
- Use quality audio equipment
- Ask open-ended questions
- Allow storyteller to lead
- Store securely with permission
- Credit storyteller appropriately
