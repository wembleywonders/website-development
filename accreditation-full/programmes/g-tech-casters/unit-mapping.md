# 🎙️ G-Tech Casters — OCN Unit Mapping Document

## Programme Overview

**Programme Name**: G-Tech Casters  
**Tagline**: "Your Voice. Your Platform. Your Story."  
**Target Age**: 11-24  
**Delivery Mode**: Blended (studio sessions + online production)  
**Platform Integration**: Rayd-yo Community Radio  

## Qualification Pathway

```
Podcast Explorer Badge (Explorer)
        ↓
Producer Badge (Builder)
        ↓
Broadcaster Badge (Innovator)
        ↓
Media Leader Badge (Leader)
        ↓
OCN Award in Creative Media Production (Level 1) — 6 credits
        ↓
OCN Certificate in Digital Media (Level 2) — 18 credits
        ↓
Content Creator Apprenticeship (Level 3)
```

## Equipment & Resources

### Core Equipment (per learner/session)
- USB microphone (Rode NT-USB / Blue Yeti)
- Headphones (closed-back)
- Pop filter
- Boom arm or mic stand

### Studio Equipment
- Audio interface (4+ channels)
- Mixer for live shows
- Acoustic treatment
- Broadcast software (Mixxx / RadioDJ)

### Software
- Audacity (free, open source)
- Anchor / Spotify for Podcasters
- Canva (artwork)
- Buffer / Later (social scheduling)

## Quality Assurance

### Assessor Requirements
- Minimum Level 3 qualification in Media Production or related field
- CAVA or equivalent assessor qualification
- Industry experience in audio/podcast production

### Internal Verification
- Sample 25% of all assessments
- Standardisation meetings quarterly
- Content quality review panel

### Evidence Storage
- Audio files on platform
- Portfolio evidence in learner dashboard
- Assessment records retained for 3 years
