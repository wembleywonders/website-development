# ♻️ Scrap Cat — Evidence Requirements

## Overview

This guide explains what evidence learners need to collect for each badge and how to submit it for assessment.

---

## Evidence Collection Principles

1. **Authenticity**: All evidence must be the learner's own work
2. **Currency**: Evidence should be recent (within 6 months)
3. **Sufficiency**: Enough evidence to demonstrate competence
4. **Variety**: Multiple evidence types strengthen submissions

---

## Badge 1: Device Diagnostic (Explorer)

### Required Evidence

| Evidence Type | Description | Format | Quantity |
|---------------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Device Identification Worksheet | Completed template identifying device types and components | PDF or photo | 1 (5+ devices) |
| Diagnostic Log | Record of fault identification exercises | Digital log or PDF | 3 entries minimum |
| Photo Evidence | Images of diagnostic process | JPEG/PNG | 3-5 photos |
| Assessor Observation Record | Completed by assessor during practical session | Signed form | 1 |

## Badge 3: Fixer (Innovator)

### Required Evidence

| Evidence Type | Description | Format | Quantity |
|---------------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Independent Repair Portfolio | Comprehensive record of solo repairs | PDF portfolio | 10 repairs |
| Video Walkthrough | Recorded repair with narration | MP4/YouTube | 1 (5-10 mins) |
| Mentoring Log | Record of mentoring session(s) | Digital log | 1+ sessions |
| Mentee Feedback | Feedback from person mentored | Completed form | 1 |
| Repair Café Certificate | Participation evidence | Certificate/photo | 1 |
| Sustainability Impact Statement | Written reflection on environmental impact | PDF/Word | 500+ words |

### Portfolio Structure

```
FIXER PORTFOLIO

SECTION 1: REPAIR EVIDENCE (10 repairs)
For each repair include:
- Repair summary (1 page)
- Before photo
- After photo
- Fault analysis
- Solution implemented
- Time taken
- Difficulty rating (1-5)
- Learning reflection

SECTION 2: VIDEO WALKTHROUGH
- Link to video
- Transcript/summary
- Technical accuracy self-check

SECTION 3: MENTORING EVIDENCE
- Session plan
- Mentoring log
- Mentee feedback form
- Self-reflection on mentoring

SECTION 4: COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
- Repair Café attendance proof
- Repairs completed at event
- Public engagement evidence

SECTION 5: SUSTAINABILITY
- Impact statement
- Calculated metrics:
  * Devices saved from landfill
  * Estimated CO2 saved
  * Money saved for owners
```

### Video Walkthrough Requirements

Video must include:
- Clear introduction of the device and fault
- Explanation of diagnostic approach
- Step-by-step repair process with narration
- Safety considerations mentioned
- Testing and verification
- Summary of outcome

Technical requirements:
- Minimum 720p resolution
- Clear audio
- 5-10 minutes duration
- Subtitles/captions (recommended)

## Submission Process

### Digital Portfolio Submission

1. Compile all evidence into organized folder structure
2. Complete Evidence Checklist (provided)
3. Submit via platform upload or email to assessor
4. Allow 10 working days for assessment
5. Receive feedback and outcome notification

### Evidence Checklist

Before submission, confirm:
- ☐ All required evidence types included
- ☐ Evidence is clearly labelled
- ☐ Photos are clear and relevant
- ☐ Written work is spell-checked
- ☐ All forms are signed where required
- ☐ Video links are accessible
- ☐ File sizes are reasonable (<50MB per file)

---

## Evidence Storage

All evidence is stored securely and retained for:
- **Active learners**: Duration of programme + 1 year
- **Completed learners**: 3 years post-completion
- **OCN certification**: As per OCN requirements
