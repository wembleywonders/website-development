# Appeals Procedure

## Overview

This procedure outlines how learners can appeal assessment decisions at Wembley Wonders CIC. We are committed to fair, transparent, and timely resolution of appeals.

---

## Grounds for Appeal

A learner may appeal if they believe:

1. **Assessment procedure** was not followed correctly
2. **Assessment criteria** were not applied fairly
3. **Reasonable adjustments** were not properly applied
4. **Evidence** was not fully considered
5. **Bias or discrimination** affected the decision

**Note**: Disagreement with an assessment outcome is not, by itself, grounds for appeal. Appeals must relate to process or fairness, not simply the result.

---

## Stage 1: Informal Resolution

### Process
1. Learner discusses concerns with their assessor
2. Assessor explains assessment decision
3. If evidence was missed, assessor may review
4. Resolution documented

### Timeframe
- Must be raised within **5 working days** of result
- Resolution within **5 working days**

### Outcomes
- Decision confirmed with explanation
- Assessment reviewed and potentially amended
- Escalation to Stage 2 if unresolved

---

## Stage 2: Formal Appeal

### Process
1. Learner submits **Appeal Form** to IQA
2. IQA reviews:
   - Original evidence
   - Assessment decision
   - Assessment records
   - Learner's grounds for appeal
3. IQA may interview assessor and learner
4. IQA makes decision
5. Written outcome provided

### Timeframe
- Appeal submitted within **10 working days** of Stage 1
- IQA decision within **10 working days** of receipt

### Outcomes
- Appeal upheld (reassessment or result change)
- Appeal partially upheld (specific actions)
- Appeal not upheld (original decision stands)

---

## Stage 3: Panel Review

### When Used
If learner remains dissatisfied after Stage 2

### Process
1. Learner requests panel review in writing
2. Panel convened (3 members):
   - Centre Lead (chair)
   - Independent assessor (different programme)
   - External representative (if available)
3. Panel reviews all documentation
4. Learner may present their case
5. Panel makes final decision

### Timeframe
- Request within **10 working days** of Stage 2
- Panel hearing within **20 working days**
- Decision within **5 working days** of hearing

### Outcomes
- Panel decision is final internally
- Learner may escalate to OCN London if still dissatisfied

---

## Stage 4: OCN London Escalation

If the learner believes Wembley Wonders has not followed proper procedures, they may escalate to OCN London.

### Contact
- Email: complaints@ocnlondon.org.uk
- Must provide evidence of completing internal stages

## Confidentiality

All appeal information is:
- Treated confidentially
- Shared only with those involved
- Stored securely
- Retained for 3 years

---

## No Detriment

Learners will not be disadvantaged for making an appeal. All appeals are handled fairly regardless of outcome.

---

## Records

All appeals are recorded including:
- Nature of appeal
- Stages completed
- Outcome at each stage
- Time taken
- Actions arising

Records reviewed quarterly to identify patterns.
