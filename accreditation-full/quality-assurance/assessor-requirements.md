# Assessor Requirements

## Overview

This document outlines the requirements for assessors delivering OCN-accredited qualifications through Wembley Wonders CIC programmes.

---

## Minimum Qualifications

### Subject Expertise

Assessors must hold:
- **Minimum Level 3 qualification** in the subject area they assess, OR
- **Equivalent industry experience** (5+ years) with documented CPD

### Assessor Qualification

Assessors must hold one of:
- **CAVA** (Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement)
- **A1/D32/D33** (legacy assessor awards)
- **Level 3 Award in Assessing Competence** (or equivalent)

### Additional Requirements

- DBS Enhanced certificate (for working with under-18s)
- Safeguarding training (Level 2 minimum)
- Understanding of equality and diversity

## Assessor Responsibilities

### Before Assessment
- Review learner evidence against criteria
- Plan assessment activities
- Prepare feedback documentation
- Ensure assessment conditions are appropriate

### During Assessment
- Apply assessment criteria consistently
- Make fair and objective judgements
- Document assessment decisions
- Provide constructive feedback

### After Assessment
- Complete assessment records
- Submit for internal verification
- Store evidence securely
- Support appeals if required

---

## Continuing Professional Development

Assessors must:
- Maintain current industry knowledge
- Attend standardisation meetings (quarterly)
- Complete annual CPD (minimum 30 hours)
- Stay updated on OCN requirements

---

## Assessor Code of Conduct

Assessors will:
- Act with integrity and impartiality
- Maintain confidentiality
- Declare conflicts of interest
- Treat learners with respect
- Follow all policies and procedures
- Report concerns appropriately

---

## Training Pathway for New Assessors

```
1. Shadow experienced assessor (2-3 assessments)
        ↓
2. Complete CAVA or equivalent
        ↓
3. Conduct supervised assessments (3-5)
        ↓
4. Internal verification of decisions
        ↓
5. Full assessor approval
```

---

## Quality Checks

All assessors subject to:
- Regular observation by IQA
- Sampling of assessment decisions
- Learner feedback review
- Annual appraisal
