# Digital Art on a Budget

## A Wembley Wonders Guide for Aspiring Digital Artists

---

## You Don't Need Expensive Gear to Start

Let's be real: the digital art world can feel like a rich person's hobby. iPads, Wacom tablets, Adobe subscriptions — it adds up fast.

**Good news:** You can create professional-quality digital art for under £50, or even completely free.

This guide shows you how to get started with what you have, build skills that transfer to earning, and access equipment through Wembley Wonders when you're ready to level up.

---

## Start Where You Are

### Level 0: Paper and Pencil (£0)

Don't skip this. Foundational drawing skills transfer directly to digital:

- Line control
- Perspective
- Shading
- Composition
- Anatomy (if drawing people)

**If you're brand new to art:** Spend time here first. Digital tools don't make you a better artist — they just change your medium.

### Level 1: Your Phone (£0)

Yes, really. People create amazing art on phones. It's not ideal, but it removes all barriers.

**Free apps:**

- **Ibis Paint X** (Android/iOS) — Full-featured, ad-supported, used by professionals
- **Sketchbook** (Android/iOS) — Clean interface, good for beginners
- **MediBang Paint** (Android/iOS) — Comic-focused, cloud sync

**Tips for phone drawing:**

- Use a cheap capacitive stylus (£3-5 from Poundland/Amazon)
- Zoom in to work on details
- Take breaks — small screens strain your eyes
- Export your work regularly (cloud backup)

## Hardware: What You Actually Need

### The Basics

To do digital art, you need:

1. **Something to draw on** (tablet, phone, or touchscreen)
2. **Something to draw with** (stylus/pen)
3. **Software** (covered above — use free options)

## Krita: Getting Started

### Installation

1. Go to [krita.org](https://krita.org)
2. Click "Download"
3. Choose your operating system
4. Install like any other software
5. It's completely free — no account, no trial, no catch

### First Steps in Krita

**When you open Krita:**

1. **Create new document:** File → New → Choose a preset (try "A4 300dpi" for print or "Screen 1920x1080" for digital)

2. **Key tools to learn first:**
   - Brush tool (B) — Your main drawing tool
   - Eraser (E) — Or hold Ctrl while brushing
   - Move tool (T) — Move layers around
   - Colour picker (P) — Pick colours from canvas

3. **Layers are essential:**
   - Think of them as transparent sheets stacked on top of each other
   - Sketch on one layer, ink on another, colour on a third
   - Layers panel is on the right

4. **Brush presets:**
   - Krita comes with hundreds of free brushes
   - Start with "Basic-5 Size" for sketching
   - "Ink" brushes for clean lines
   - "Airbrush" for soft shading
