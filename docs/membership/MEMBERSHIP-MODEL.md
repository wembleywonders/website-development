# Wembley Wonders Family Membership Model

## The Problem We're Solving

| Private Tuition Reality | Cost Per Child | Cost for 2 Children | Cost for 3 Children |
| ----------------------- | -------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Music only | £1,200-1,440/year | £2,400-2,880/year | £3,600-4,320/year |
| Music + Maths | £2,160-2,640/year | £4,320-5,280/year | £6,480-7,920/year |
| Music + Maths + English | £3,120-3,840/year | £6,240-7,680/year | £9,360-11,520/year |
| Full coverage (4 subjects) | £4,080-5,040/year | £8,160-10,080/year | £12,240-15,120/year |

**The bigger the family, the more impossible traditional tuition becomes.**

---

## Our Solution: Family-Based Pricing

### Principle 1: Unlimited Children Per Household

A family with 5 children pays the same as a family with 1 child at every tier.

**Why:**

- Large families need support most
- Per-child pricing punishes families for having children
- Community benefit increases with more children engaged

### Principle 2: Sliding Scale, Not Fixed Prices

No family turned away for inability to pay. Those who can pay more, subsidise those who can't.

### Principle 3: Value Transparency

Every family sees exactly what equivalent private tuition would cost. They understand the value they're receiving.

## Membership Tiers

### 🌟 COMMUNITY TIER (FREE)

**Who it's for:** Families who cannot afford any contribution

**What's included:**

- Full access to all digital tools
  - Trubble n Bass Studio
  - Bright Sparks learning games
  - PageTurners writing tools
  - STEMgeneers coding environment
  - G-Tech Casters podcast tools
  - All other programme sandboxes
- Self-guided learning pathways
- Community forums and peer support
- Basic progress tracking
- **Unlimited children per household**

**Value delivered:** £3,000-5,000/year per child (equivalent tuition)

**Contribution:** £0

---

### ⭐ SUPPORTER TIER (£10-20/month sliding scale)

**Who it's for:** Families who can contribute something

**Suggested contribution:**

- £10/month if tight
- £15/month if managing
- £20/month if comfortable

**Everything in Community, plus:**

- Priority workshop/event booking
- Monthly 1-on-1 mentor check-in (rotating between children)
- Detailed progress reports (printable)
- Certificate printing and verification
- Access to equipment loan programme waitlist
- **Unlimited children per household**

**Value delivered:** £3,000-5,000/year per child + £500-800/year mentor time

**Contribution:** £120-240/year total (regardless of number of children)

---

### 💎 PATRON TIER (£50+/month - pay what you feel)

**Who it's for:** Families who can afford more and want to support others

**Everything in Supporter, plus:**

- Weekly mentor sessions (can split between children)
- Exam preparation support (Grade prep, 11+, GCSE support)
- Equipment loan programme priority access
- Named recognition as community patron
- **Your contribution sponsors 2 Community families**
- **Unlimited children per household**

**Value delivered:** £3,000-5,000/year per child + £1,500-2,000/year mentor/exam prep

**Contribution:** £600+/year (supports your family + 2 others)

## Value Calculation Methodology

We calculate equivalent tuition value based on:

| Programme | Equivalent Subject | Hourly Rate | Source |
| --------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ------- |
| Trubble n Bass | Music tuition | £55/hr | Average Brent music tutor rates |
| Bright Sparks | Primary maths/science | £45/hr | Primary tuition rates |
| STEMgeneers | STEM tuition | £50/hr | Specialist STEM rates |
| PageTurners | English tuition | £45/hr | English tutor rates |
| G-Tech Casters | Communication/media | £40/hr | Specialist coaching |
| Kaywana's Court | Drama/performance | £50/hr | Drama school rates |
| TECHreneurs | Business coaching | £45/hr | Youth enterprise rates |
| Money Reset | Financial literacy | £40/hr | Financial coaching rates |

**Calculation:** Hours spent × Equivalent hourly rate = Tuition value

## The Dashboard Promise

Every parent can see:

1. **Hours logged** - per child, per programme
2. **Equivalent value** - "This month: £X in tuition value"
3. **Progress made** - levels, skills, achievements
4. **What they'd pay privately** - clear comparison
5. **What they actually paid** - their contribution
6. **Their savings** - the difference

This isn't marketing. It's accountability. They can verify the value themselves.
