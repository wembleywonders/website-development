# Soul — Direct Coaching & Platform-Building with Sam Cooke
## Leader Tier: Running It at SAR's Scale

*Tutor: Sam Cooke — co-founded SAR Records in 1961 specifically to give Black artists a venue during the racially charged 1960s. Tirelessly coached vocalists during SAR sessions, emphasising diction; audible on surviving tape coaching artists between takes. Bobby Womack, who came up under him: "by him being there with you and just giving you that special attention, you wanted to give it to him like it was supposed to be. I think that's what made SAR, SAR."*

---

### Before you start

Innovator tier proved the convergence — one coaching relationship producing one earned placement. But SAR wasn't a single success. It was a real, ongoing label, sustaining that same convergence across multiple artists at once, each at a different stage of readiness. That's the actual scale Cooke operated at, and it's a genuinely different challenge than repeating Innovator tier's single case: with more than one person, you can't give everyone the same attention at the same time, and pretending otherwise is how coaching quality quietly degrades across a roster.

---

### 1. What changes when it's more than one person

With one developing artist, all your attention goes to a single thread — you can track exactly where they are and what they need next. With several, the real skill is triage: recognising which relationship needs your attention *now*, without abandoning the others entirely or spreading yourself so thin that none of them gets real coaching. This is a different judgment than Innovator tier's single-thread readiness question — it's about honest resource allocation across real, competing claims on your time.

### 2. Why a platform can't showcase everyone at once, either

The same discipline applies on the Platform-Building side. A label with five artists doesn't release five debut singles the same week — it sequences them, based on genuine readiness, not fairness or favouritism. Cooke's own SAR roster had artists at different stages; the platform served each one when their specific work was actually ready, not on a fixed schedule. Sequencing honestly, rather than either playing favourites or trying to force simultaneous launches, is itself part of this tier's craft.

### 3. Why "I'm coaching five people" isn't automatically Leader-tier evidence

Scale on its own isn't the achievement — sustained quality across scale is. Five shallow coaching relationships and five vague platform mentions would technically be "more than one," but wouldn't demonstrate anything Innovator tier didn't already require, done worse and thinner. The actual test: can you show, for at least two different artists at genuinely different stages, the same causal rigor Innovator tier required for one — real coaching that specifically earned a real, evidenced placement?

### Try This Yourself (Leader tier evidence)

1. **Identify at least two artists at genuinely different stages** — one closer to readiness, one earlier in development. Don't pick two people at the same stage; the triage judgment this tier is testing only shows up when the stages actually differ.
2. **Log real coaching for both**, honestly reflecting the different attention each actually needs right now — not identical session counts for the sake of symmetry.
3. **Make at least one real placement**, for whichever artist has genuinely earned it, with the same causal link Innovator tier required (name the specific coaching that made the placement deserved). For the other artist, write an honest account of where they stand and why they're not there yet.
4. **Write a short triage reflection**: how did you decide where your attention went this cycle, and what did the other artist temporarily go without? Honesty about the tradeoff is the actual evidence here, not a claim that both got equal attention.

Submit both coaching records, the placement (with its causal link) for whoever earned it, the honest "not yet" account for the other, and your triage reflection together as your Leader-tier evidence.

## Self-Check Before Submitting

- Are your two artists genuinely at different stages, or did you pick two similar cases to avoid the real triage question?
- Does your placement still have the same causal rigor Innovator tier required, or did scale quietly loosen that standard?
- Did you write honestly about what the less-attended artist went without, rather than implying both got equal treatment?
