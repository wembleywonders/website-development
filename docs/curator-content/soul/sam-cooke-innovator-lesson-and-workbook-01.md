# Soul — Direct Coaching & Platform-Building with Sam Cooke
## Innovator Tier: Where the Two Tracks Meet

*Tutor: Sam Cooke — co-founded SAR Records in 1961 specifically to give Black artists a venue during the racially charged 1960s. Tirelessly coached vocalists during SAR sessions, emphasising diction; audible on surviving tape coaching artists between takes. Bobby Womack, who came up under him: "by him being there with you and just giving you that special attention, you wanted to give it to him like it was supposed to be. I think that's what made SAR, SAR."*

---

### Before you start — why this tier can't be split into two tracks again

Explorer and Builder tier deliberately trained Coaching and Platform-Building as two separate tracks — Cooke's own history bundles two distinct skills, and Builder tier's rule was to run each one's full solo-then-real structure separately so neither got shortchanged. Innovator tier is different on purpose: this is where the two tracks actually have to work as one, because that's what SAR itself was. Bobby Womack didn't get vocal notes from Cooke *and separately* get a label deal — he got a label willing to put out a record because the coaching had already made the work good enough to put out. Splitting the tracks again here would misrepresent the real technique at exactly the tier where it matters most.

---

### 1. What "convergence" actually requires, beyond doing both

It's not enough to coach someone and, separately, offer them a platform slot. The convergence is causal: the coaching has to be the reason the platform placement is genuine rather than premature. A platform-building act that showcases work nobody actually developed toward readiness isn't SAR's method — it's just promotion. The test: could you say, honestly, that the specific coaching you gave is what made this specific placement deserved, not just possible?

### 2. Why premature platforming is its own real failure

There's a temptation, once you have any platform to offer, to use it quickly — a share, a feature, a slot filled. Cooke's own discipline cuts against that: SAR's credibility depended on what it put out actually being ready, not just available. A platform that features unready work loses the thing that makes a platform valuable in the first place — the audience's trust that what's featured there has actually been developed. Rushing the placement to "complete" this tier would be exactly the failure this tier exists to prevent, not a shortcut through it.

### 3. Why this is the hardest tier for this curator specifically

Builder tier's two tracks were both real but independently achievable — you could complete real coaching sessions on one track and a real platform-building act on the other without either depending on the other's outcome. Innovator tier removes that independence deliberately. If the coaching doesn't actually work, there's no honest placement to make — and the honest response is the same one Dodd's Innovator tier names directly: recognising that a relationship hasn't reached readiness yet is real evidence of the craft, not a failure to hide.

### Try This Yourself (Innovator tier evidence)

1. **Pick one specific member's work you've already coached** (from Builder tier, or a continuation of that relationship) that has reached genuine readiness — not just "some sessions happened," but a real, specific improvement you can name, confirmed by the member themselves or a facilitator.
2. **Make the placement, and make the causal link explicit.** Offer that specific work a real platform slot — a feature, a share, a listing — and write, plainly, why the coaching is what made this placement deserved now rather than earlier. Name the specific change that crossed the line from "not ready" to "ready."
3. **If the work genuinely isn't ready yet**, write that honestly instead of forcing a placement — name what's missing and what coaching would still need to happen. This is a complete, valid Innovator-tier submission on its own terms, the same principle Dodd's Innovator tier applies to knowing when to walk away.
4. **Track the reach, honestly.** If you did make the placement: how do you know it reached someone — a listen count, a direct response, a follow-on booking? Vague optimism isn't evidence here any more than it was at Builder tier.

Submit the coaching record, the placement (or the honest "not yet ready" account), and the reach evidence together as your Innovator-tier evidence.
