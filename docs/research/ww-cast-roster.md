## Connected characters — all eight now fully formed (27 Aug 2026)

### Reece

Formation drafted 18 Aug 2026. Young Black man, genuinely sharp with
money — good at spotting margin, reading people, timing risk, real aptitude
currently pointed at the wrong ledger rather than absent. Works at/hangs
around Don's Unit already (not choosing between two separate locations) —
the season's "easy money vs steady income" dilemma is deliberately NOT
framed as road vs shop, since that framing was corrected mid-development:
the real axis is fast-dishonest vs slow-honest money, and that fork exists
inside legitimate-looking trades too, not just on the road.

Core dramatic engine: a road contact offers Reece money to move stolen
catalytic converters through Don's Unit (or similar — cash-in-hand off the
books, parts with no provenance, "no questions asked" jobs), a real and
well-documented UK crime pattern. This makes Don's Unit an actively-
maintained honest economy rather than an automatically-pure moral safe
harbour — Don and Kofi's business stays clean because they keep refusing
that offer, not because a shopfront makes temptation impossible; nobody in
this thread gets to be the flawless example. Counter-models Reece weighs:
Babatunde's decades of patient trust-built economy at The Kola House, and
Don/Kofi's real working trade — two live alternatives in view, not an
abstract "good path" lecture. CJ's own line flagged as strong in-voice
dialogue for Babatunde or Don to actually say: "Variety may be the spice of
life, but spice is not the same as substance." Resolution deliberately left
open per CJ's "let the reader decide" standard — a single small step toward
the honest economy, not a completed redemption arc in one episode.

**Season-arc framing** (per CJ, 18 Aug 2026): all of this season's separate
episodes — the kaffir/Libya thread, the dougla/bleaching-cream thread, the
ISoc gender-segregation thread (Fatima), and Reece's thread — share one
throughline: people whose own communities/institutions fail them, seeking
real solutions outside the expected circle. Auntie Jenny's visit to The
Kola House was the first instance of this pattern (seeking what the
Wembley Central boy's own mosque couldn't give him); Reece's thread is the
same shape applied to economic rather than religious/communal belonging.

**Economic escalation added** (18 Aug 2026): "spice is not substance" given
a real financial mechanism, not just a moral one — dishonest income isn't
free once concealment costs are counted; "eventually you're going to have
to pay extra for a creative book-keeper or a persuasive barrister" names
the compounding cost structure directly. CJ confirmed (19 Aug 2026)
positive reaction to a drafted Don's Unit scene where Don addresses Reece's
road-contact temptation without lecturing — door-always-open, "I know
what's been offered, I'm not going to pretend I don't, but the offer
doesn't run out on a deadline" register — specifically praising how
positive and forward-looking it read; confirms this as the tone to keep
for Don/Reece mentorship beats going forward, distinct from
surveillance/suspicion framing.

**Elder-figure scene registers** (standing principle, 20 Aug 2026, per
CJ): confirmed as a pattern worth drawing on rather than reinventing each
time, not a single house style. Auntie Jenny specifically established as
the unflinching/hard-truth register (a drafted Myrtle's Kitchen scene with
Sandra, built around CJ's own grandmother's proverb about being too choosy
young and ending up choosing from desperation later, has Auntie Jenny
deliver the hard truth once, plainly, without chasing Sandra's reaction or
softening the landing afterward — comfort is in the honesty itself and the
door staying open, not in cushioning the message), distinct from Don's
patient/open-ended register with Reece. CJ's framing: "you can come here
without judgement but know that you're not always going to get the answer
you expect." CJ explicitly cautioned against flattening this into one tone
across all elder-younger pairings — emotional register should vary by
person, relationship, and situation, since each character grows
differently in their own experience and understanding; sometimes it plays
as a clash of ideas, sometimes as one side giving way, sometimes as
agreement on shared/mutual principles, and these shouldn't be homogenized
scene to scene.

**Valentine** — already established in canon as Streetbeats' "dark
mirror," the book-cooking accountant — identified as the natural existing
endpoint if Reece's road-money ever needs cleaning, rather than inventing a
new character; ties Reece's arc into existing canon instead of adding to
it. The "persuasive barrister" side **decided** (18 Aug 2026): Sandra
(Fernandez family, established left-wing law graduate) confirmed rather
than left unfilled — reconsidered from initial read of "doesn't fit
cleanly." Fit works once framed correctly: not a generic criminal-defence
barrister, but Sandra's already-established civil-rights politics extended
into defending people the system fails, which is the season's own
throughline (institutions fail people, they seek solutions outside them)
played out from inside the law rather than around it. Creates real sibling
tension: Valentine confirmed (18 Aug 2026, per CJ) as Sandra's brother, not
just co-listed in the same family grouping — Valentine enables concealment,
Sandra defends against consequences, both working the same family fault
line from opposite sides. Also consistent with Sandra's established
resourceful use of legal/IP knowledge (the Steps shop/Clifton copyright
manoeuvre) — foreshadows rather than contradicts this extension.

**Full anti-hero philosophy added** (18 Aug 2026, per CJ): Valentine's
internal logic — if the big players cheat the system as standard practice,
refusing to use that same leverage for people who actually need it isn't
integrity, it's unilateral disarmament; his "creative book-keeping" is
framed by him as the only realistic route out of the poverty trap for his
community, applied "judiciously," not simple greed. Sandra's internal
logic — the legal system is rigged against the poor and vulnerable, so her
passionate advocacy trips up police specifically on "sus law" grounds (real
historical term: the 1824 Vagrancy Act's "suspected persons" stop-and-search
provision, used disproportionately against Black communities for over a
century, the named trigger of the 1981 Brixton uprising, repealed following
the Scarman Report — real civil-rights lineage, not invented shorthand) and
pleads for lesser sentencing. Her own complicating cost, per CJ: that same
lenient-sentencing advocacy feeds a revolving door of local low-level crime
back into the community she's trying to protect. Deliberately kept as
genuinely contested rather than settled: whether lighter sentencing reduces
or enables reoffending is an actual unresolved debate in real criminology
(rehabilitation-focused vs deterrence-focused research), so neither
Valentine nor Sandra needs to be "wrong" for the tension to be real — both
anti-heroes in their own story, both coherent, both causing real harm
through what they believe is right.

### Fatima Barry

Formation drafted 18 Aug 2026. Fula/Peul heritage, surname common across
Guinea/Sierra Leone/Senegal/Mali; family line traces through Sierra Leone
rather than direct from Guinea, tying her to Krio/Freetown migration
history distinct from the cast's Caribbean-heritage majority. Family
Islamic tradition runs centuries deep and scholarly — echoes of the Fouta
Djallon Islamic states/Sokoto Caliphate lineage — not a recent-conversion
or imposed-identity story; first-generation British-born, first in her
family at university, so real domestic stakes attach to any position she
takes rather than staying abstract.

Reads Law (equality law interest specifically), which gives her literal
proximity to the EHRC gender-segregation guidance her ISoc storyline turns
on, and echoes her family's own tradition of textual/religious scholarship
channelled into a secular register — same instinct for structured
argument, different subject. Personality: precise, doesn't perform
certainty she doesn't have, genuinely holds the traditionalist and
reformer positions in tension rather than resolving them for an audience —
she understands both from the inside, not as a detached mediator.
Criminal-law specialism confirmed 27 Aug 2026 (differentiates her from
Sandra's family-law specialism, sharpening what was flagged as a real
clustering overlap between the two — both remain legally-trained and
civil-rights-coded, but now work different day-to-day terrain: Sandra's
domestic/relational, Fatima's public/courtroom/community-facing). Voice
register: formal but warm, careful word choice from legal training,
Krio/Pular phrasing surfaces around family/informal moments.

Badge-pathway connection: Bright Sparks alum (drawn in via the genealogy
taster) who graduated into Roots to research her own family's Fula
scholarly lineage — real, rich, verifiable material — which can feed
Pageturners per the existing Roots→Pageturners heritage-vignette pairing.
Street connection: appears at The Kola House as a customer, not family —
gives Babatunde a second reason to exist on the street beyond the

different West African-heritage characters rather than one storyline

## Horatio Altmeister — full character development

- **Epithet**: "Greedy Anansi" — the trickster tradition (wit over force,
  for the community) corrupted and turned against it; he'd recognise and
  enjoy the resemblance in himself.
- **Archetype blend**: Flashman (Jennings and Darbisher) as the
  psychological spine — cowardice dressed as boldness, charm as the actual
  weapon, always engineers situations where he gets credit and someone
  else takes the fall, genuinely believes his own cover story after
  repeating it enough. Political-operative texture layered on top —
  cynical, procedurally fluent, a "coup-shaped instinct for power" (thinks
  in factions/leverage/who's-loyal-to-whom) — built as entirely invented
  character traits, explicitly NOT scaffolded on any real identifiable
  person (Bernie Grant considered and kept only as a private, non-public
  generalised-charisma touchstone; Bernard Coard/NJM explicitly rejected as
  source material given he is a real, still-living, specifically
  identifiable person tied to real events).
- **Mantra**: "How can we sweeten the pot" — ties directly to his defining
  trait (makes an unfavourable deal sound like a favour, believes it
  himself while saying it); recurs across all six origin stages, same four
  words carrying progressively less genuine generosity and more theatre as
  he evolves.
- **Family**: white wife — married into for connections/respectability
  rather than romance, acquired the way he acquires everything; open
  question whether she ever fully clocks what she was used for. Four
  outside children — same appetite for leverage applied to people instead
  of property, accumulate from Stage 4 onward. Family confirmed as unable
  to stand his selfishness.
- **His court**: 2nd lieutenant — compliant fixer, permanently astride a
  donkey always trying to buck him off (physical comedy = character note: a
  man who thinks he's mastered his situation while visibly losing control
  of it); needs his own original name/design (Baldrick/Muttley ruled out as
  copyrighted) — named below as Alison Gate. Press officer — Brer Parrot
  (repeats Horatio's lines back louder and shinier, public-domain
  folklore). Foil — Brer Rabbit (technically in his orbit but quietly
  outsmarts him using the real trickster-through-wit tradition Horatio
  betrayed; seeds his eventual downfall). Sidekick — Brer Monkey (comic
  muscle/gopher, pure slapstick).

Staged like evolution rather than a single reveal (Penguin-in-Gotham
model), not introduced whole-cloth as a villain:

1. **Stage 1** (20s): shifty estate agent working Wembley's smaller
   shopfronts/flats — genuinely skilled at reading what people want to
   hear; morally flexible and hungry, not yet a villain. Rides in on the
   rise of Right to Buy.
2. **Stage 2**: the lesson — a specific incident where a vulnerable/elderly
   landlord (deliberately kept unconnected to existing canon characters)

   profitable route instead; origin moment reframes as "nobody was owed
   the truth in the first place" — this is where "Greedy Anansi" gets
   earned.
3. **Stage 3**: learns manufactured scarcity — a shopfront left
   deliberately empty is worth more than one doing business; small-scale
   root of the Metropole-squeeze tactic already in canon. Sharpened

   converting to multi-occupancy for yield).

   real thread of ancestry stretched and repeated until it hardens into
   fact in his own mouth; four outside children start accumulating.
5. **Stage 5**: Crown Gate Development formalises as an institution —

   high-rise overlooking Wembley Stadium, where he "entertains." Has Tinder

6. **Stage 6** (present): public "227 years" magnate; privately running the
   same con learned at twenty-five. Auntie Jenny's Archivist thread

   exposes the gap between the manufactured claim and reality.

## Four-faction animal-kingdom power structure

These compete and cooperate warily with each other, always seeking
advantage, while Easy Street's human community leaders try to dodge the
fallout.

- **The Stadium** — old power, "more native"/established, doesn't need to
  hustle. Members: Brer Rabbit (chairman of the board), Brer Monkey (his
  uncanny deputy, always two steps ahead).
- **The Imports** — diaspora strivers, "stolen from Africa, trying to make
  their mark in the new world." Horatio/Anansi's own faction. Members:
  Horatio, his 2nd lieutenant Alison Gate, Brer Parrot (press officer).
- **The Press** — "Who Dead" local rag staff. **JR the Jaguar** —
  proprietor/top of the masthead, "the indomitable one nobody wants to
  cross, rules the forest," ultimate editorial call on what runs.
  **Jeffries the Anteater** — the goss journalist "everyone reads for the
  dirt on the foibles of the great and the good," does Horatio's flattering
  pitch stories. **Carole the Capybara** — Jeffries' love interest, covers
  human-interest stories (fetes, fairs, red-carpet events), "deep in the
  ladies circle" — has real social access/trust Jeffries doesn't.
- **The Council** — "all the great and good of the animal kingdom,"
  civic/regulatory establishment faction — membership not yet populated.

Thematic throughline for "Who Dead" confirmed: "it's a jungle out there,"
casualties are everyone's concern across all four factions, and it's apt
that only JR the Jaguar survives to tell the tale — the Press outlives
every scheme it reports on rather than winning its own.

**2nd lieutenant named**: Alison Gate (Alligator) — patient, cunning, never
forgets, rarely lets anything escape her; rides the donkey that's always
trying to buck her off, but her stillness never breaks — the only member of
Horatio's own court who's genuinely as formidable as he performs being.

**Appearance-frequency principle** confirmed for the four-faction cast:
power and screen presence run inversely, matching real institutions —
top-tier figures (chairman/Brer Rabbit, and by extension figures like the
Mayor) are rarely seen directly, appearing at most once or twice at
ceremonies/meetings or via a sent proxy with a proposal, while their
deputies/working-level counterparts (Brer Monkey, Beaver, Tortoise) carry
the actual recurring on-screen relationship with Horatio.

**Fourth pantheon tier** proposed, above the mythic monsters: "the Major
Arcana — the deities" — bless or punish according to behaviour,
predict/prophesy, are consulted and venerated, rarely ignored; travel by
legend rather than direct appearance, stories retold of creation and
destruction. Same "appear only when the story requires it" discipline as
the other tiers, but functioning as the highest/rarest register.

**Original invented pantheon confirmed** (not real Orisha/Loa figures) —
informed by that register but distinct, same treatment as
Flashman/J.R. Ewing/Coard precedent this session; documented trail
deliberately left in the Knowledge Commons for anyone who wants to trace
the real-world influences, but nothing claims to depict a real living
tradition.

Domain structure confirmed: three-deity/three-cluster shape
(Making/Keeping/Community, mapped onto the existing 12-Children-of-Anansi
clusters) — CJ's own call that going beyond three would feel leaden,
