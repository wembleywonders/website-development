## OPEN RESEARCH ITEM — register cross-check

**RESOLVED 16 Sep 2026 (Claude Code).** Full evidence and citations in
`docs/research/WW-SPEC-KC-REGISTER-CROSSCHECK-001.md` — summary here:

Pettigrew's "Register of British Slave Traders" (BBC Sep 2026 coverage,
~13,000 names, South Sea Company branding-iron evidence) and "the City of
London" leaked ~280-name report are **not the same project**, but they
**are outputs of the same wider Pettigrew-led research programme** — a
distinction the original filing didn't have the sourcing to draw:

- **Register of British Slave Traders** — AHRC-funded (~£1m, formal grant
  title "The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade and the Development of Europe"),
  Pettigrew (KCL, PI) + Radburn (Lancaster) + Smith (Manchester) + Draper
  (UCL), started 2021. **Publicly live since 14 September 2026** at
  britishslavetraders.org. Owns the South Sea Company evidence — the
  entry already logged against this project's provenance is correct, no
  correction needed.
- **The leaked ~280-name report** is a separate, smaller commission
  (£34,400) directly from the City of London Corporation to Pettigrew,
  titled "Assessing the Connections between the Transatlantic Traffic in
  Enslaved African People and the City of London Corporation," dated 18
  May 2026, scope 1640–1807. Still only a leaked draft; Corporation says
  a formal published version is coming "later in 2026," no date yet.
- **A third report, newly found this pass and not previously tracked
  anywhere:** "Report assessing the connections between the transatlantic
  traffic in enslaved African people and the Bridge House Estates,"
  same author, commissioned by the City Bridge Foundation, **already
  published** (foreword dated November 2025).
