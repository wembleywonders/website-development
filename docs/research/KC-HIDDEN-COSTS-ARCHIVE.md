### Video 1 — Used-boat depreciation `[SCRAP CAT] [STEMGENEERS/NEVILLE] [TECHRENEURS]`

15 categories of boats now worth scrap-to-nothing: stern-drive cruisers losing value to
outboard-market shift and driveline service costs; OMC-drive boats orphaned by OMC's
2000 bankruptcy; pre-1984 fiberglass fuel tanks dissolving under ethanol gas; carbureted
two-stroke outboards being banned on various lakes; boats over ~35-40 years old routinely
refused insurance regardless of condition; "laundered" storm-damaged boats resold with
clean titles in most US states; oversupplied 1970s-80s production sailboats;
houseboats/kit boats broadly uninsurable; older model lines devalued by rapid new-model
turnover; boats with discontinued proprietary digital helm systems; disposal/salvage
costs alone running $10,000-40,000+.

Central mechanism: insurers' "agreed value" clauses can depreciate hard-goods payout
~10%/year past 20 years of age while premiums stay flat, undisclosed unless the buyer
specifically requests the schedule in writing before buying.

### Video 2 — Land-buying deal-breakers `[TECHRENEURS]`

7 categories: junk/debris removal costs escalating once excavation starts, with possible
triggered environmental remediation; boundary encroachments; unsuitable soil (denied
septic permits cutting value ~80%); protected-species habitat triggering mitigation

### Video 3 — UK woodland ownership rules `[TECHRENEURS]`

10 permitted/restricted uses: owning/roaming/28-days-camping fully permitted;
management (planting, coppicing, clearing) fully permitted subject to
nesting-season/bat-roost timing; up to 5 cubic meters of firewood per quarter without a
felling license (only 2 cubic meters may be sold); fencing/gating/small ponds permitted
within limits; access-track building usually permitted development
(UK-nation-dependent process); small forestry shelters conditionally permitted if
genuinely for forestry use, not residential; camping/caravan use beyond 28 days becomes a
blockable "material change of use"; felling protected/ancient/TPO trees can incur fines
up to £25,000 per tree plus forced replanting; running the wood as a business (events,
timber sales) permitted within caps, blocked beyond them; living there full-time is never
permitted regardless of remoteness or concealment.

cheaper to run than a cheap older one.

### Video 10 — "Enshittification" essay/build-vlog `[SCRAP CAT] [TECHRENEURS]`

Cory Doctorow's term, extended by the video's creator beyond digital platforms, arguing
structural quality decay is a consequence of growth-dependent, debt-based economics once
genuine productivity gains slow, forcing extraction via planned obsolescence,
subscriptions, and data harvesting. Framed around a personal 1991 Mercedes-Benz 190E
restoration (~$10,000 all-in, explicitly an emotional rather than financial decision).

Sourced claims cited: 1920s Phoebus cartel deliberately shortening lightbulb lifespans; a
2023 Mozilla Foundation finding that all 25 reviewed car brands failed baseline
privacy/data standards; US public-company count roughly halving 1997-2012 via
consolidation; John Deere/Samsung-TV lock-in examples.

Practical advice: check whether a company's guarantee model requires the product to
actually last; check what happens to software-dependent products if support ends; use
iFixit repairability scores; audit recurring subscriptions against what you'd actively
re-choose.

### Video 11 — Passive cooling architecture `[STEMGENEERS/NEVILLE] [TECHRENEURS]`

Centered on Hassan Fathy's 1946 New Gourna village project near Luxor, Egypt: courtyard
geometry combines night-sky radiative cooling, thermal-mass wall storage, self-shading,
and stack-effect ventilation to cool buildings with no mechanical system.

Cited studies: a 1972 Baghdad courtyard-house measurement; a review of 30 field studies
finding ~2-5°F indoor/outdoor temperature reduction from courtyard geometry alone;
Seville building studies showing 8-18% cooling-energy reduction from geometry; a 2025
machine-learning study finding the effect persists in humid climates.

Fathy's houses were criticized by Egyptian officials as "distressingly inexpensive,"
undermining a concrete-modernization prestige agenda; New Gourna was never fully
completed. US-specific argument: zoning setbacks, street-frontage rules, and
appraiser/lender/HOA comparable-sales bias structurally block courtyard construction
regardless of its physics — paralleled with minimum-dwelling-size rules blocking tiny
houses via a different rule. Notes recent US deregulation: Idaho's Senate Bill 1352
(signed April 1, 2026); an Indiana by-right housing law effective March 2026.
